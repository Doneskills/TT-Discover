Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

1. Introduction

TT Discover helps people discover businesses, explore services, and find employment opportunities in Trinidad and Tobago.

This Privacy Policy explains how TT Discover collects, uses, stores, and discloses personal information when you visit the website, create an account, manage a business page, or use other platform features.

We aim to handle personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

2. Information We Collect

A. Account information

When you create or manage an account, we collect your email address and a password (we store it scrambled, never in plain text). We also record whether you signed up as a business or for updates, and if you sign up for updates, the area you want news about.

If you choose to, you can add a username, real name, short description, and profile photo.

B. Business listings and uploaded content

When you create or manage a business page, you may submit:

  • Business names, descriptions, categories and locations.
  • Business contact details, opening hours, and links.
  • Photos, logos, menus, and other uploaded materials.
  • Deals, posts, and job listings.

Most of this information is public. Please do not submit private, confidential, or sensitive personal information unless it is necessary, lawful, and appropriate to do so.

C. Reviews and photos you post

Reviews, ratings and photos you add to a business page are public and show your username (or “Anonymous” if you have not set one). Your email address is never shown with them.

D. Messages to us

If you use the contact form, write to us, or send a note about a suspended account, we receive the message and your email address by email, and keep them as long as needed to reply, prevent abuse, and resolve disputes.

E. Technical and usage information

  • Page views: we count how many times each business page is opened, per day, so owners can see how they are doing. We do not show owners who the visitors were.
  • Hosting records: our hosting provider may automatically process technical information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as IP addresses, browser and device information, timestamps, and error or security logs.
  • Sign-in protection: we briefly use IP addresses to limit repeated sign-in and form attempts.

F. Cookies and storage on your device

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not use third-party analytics. We save a sign-in token and your dark or light mode choice in your browser's storage so you stay signed in and the site looks how you like.

3. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Display business listings and content that users choose to publish.
  • Support reviews, photos, job listings, and invitations to help manage a business page.
  • Send emails: confirming your email, resetting your password, invitations and account notices, and, if you asked for updates, news about new businesses and deals in your area.
  • Respond to enquiries and support requests.
  • Keep the site working and safe, including reviewing reports of reviews, photos, and businesses.
  • Investigate spam, fraud, abuse, or technical problems.
  • Understand website performance and improve the platform.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Public Information

Information submitted to a public business page, public job listing, deal, review, or photo may be visible to other users and may be copied, shared, indexed by search engines, or kept in third-party systems.

Before publishing content, consider whether it is appropriate for public display. Deleting a listing or account may not immediately remove copies already stored by search engines, other users, or external websites.

5. How Information May Be Shared

We do not sell your personal information. We may share or make information available in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: companies that help us run the site. These are Render (hosting), a cloud database provider (storage), and Brevo (sending email). If paid plans open, payments are handled by PayPal or WiPay, and we never see your card details. They only get what they need to do their job.
  • Other users and the public: information you choose to publish through public listings, job advertisements, deals, reviews, or photos.
  • Business pages you help manage: if you accept an invitation to help manage a business, the owner can see that you are a collaborator.
  • Legal and safety purposes: where disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to address security incidents, fraud, or protect legal rights.
  • With your direction or consent: where you request or authorise a particular disclosure.

6. Storage, Security, and International Processing

TT Discover is hosted using Render, and information is stored with a cloud database provider. Information may therefore be processed on servers outside Trinidad and Tobago.

We take reasonable steps appropriate to our resources and the information involved to protect data against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure, such as scrambling passwords and limiting sign-in attempts. We also keep backup copies of the site's data so it can be restored if something goes wrong. No website or electronic storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Please use a password you do not use anywhere else, and avoid sending sensitive information through public listings or messages.

7. How Long We Keep Information

We keep your information while your account is open and for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, subject to legal obligations and legitimate needs such as security, dispute resolution, and recordkeeping.

If you ask us to delete your account or information, we will remove what we are able and legally permitted to remove. Copies in backups may remain for a limited time before they are replaced. Reviews and photos you posted may be removed together with your account.

8. Your Choices and Requests

Subject to applicable law and the technical operation of the platform, you may contact us to request:

  • Access to personal information associated with your account.
  • Correction of inaccurate information.
  • Deletion of information that we are able and legally permitted to delete.
  • Information about how your data is used or disclosed.

You can change or stop update emails at any time using the “Stop these emails” link at the bottom of those emails, or in Settings. You can also edit your profile in your account.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

9. Children and Young Users

TT Discover is not meant for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, please contact us and we will review it and remove it where appropriate.

10. Third-Party Websites

TT Discover contains links to external websites, including business websites and social media pages. We do not control their privacy practices. When you visit a third-party website, review its own privacy policy and terms.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as TT Discover develops. We will publish the revised version and update the “Last updated” date. Where required, we will provide notice or obtain consent.
