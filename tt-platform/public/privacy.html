Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Draft: this page was written in plain language for TT Discover. Before launch, have a lawyer in Trinidad and Tobago check it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a password (we store it scrambled, never in plain text), and whether you signed up as a business or for updates.
  • Profile details you choose to add: username, real name, a short description and a profile photo.
  • Business details: if you create a business, the name, category, description, address, phone, email, hours, photos, menu, links, deals, posts and job listings you enter.
  • What you post: reviews, ratings and photos you add to a business page.
  • Your area: if you sign up for updates, the area you want news about.
  • Page views: we count how many times each business page is opened, per day, so owners can see how they are doing. We do not show owners who the visitors were.

How we use it

  • To run the site, show business pages and keep your account working.
  • To send emails: confirming your email, resetting your password, and, if you asked for updates, telling you about new businesses and deals in your area.
  • To keep the site safe, including looking at reports of reviews, photos and businesses.

What other people can see

  • Everything a business adds to its page is public.
  • Your reviews and photos are public and show your username (or "Anonymous" if you have not set one). Your email is never shown on them.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We use other companies to run the site: a hosting provider, a database provider and an email provider. If you buy Premium, payments are handled by PayPal or WiPay, and we never see your card details. These companies only get what they need to do their job.

Storage on your device

We save a sign-in token and your dark or light mode choice in your browser so you stay signed in and the site looks how you like. We do not use advertising trackers.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while your account is open. To delete your account, or to ask what we hold about you, email kendd5000@gmail.com. Reviews and photos you posted may be removed with your account.

Security

We take sensible steps to protect your information, such as scrambling passwords and limiting sign-in attempts. No website can promise perfect security, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

Children

TT Discover is not meant for children under 13. If you think a child has given us information, tell us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change this page in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top.

Contact

Questions? Email kendd5000@gmail.com.
